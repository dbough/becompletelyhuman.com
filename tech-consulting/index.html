Small jobs welcome

No job is too small.

In twenty-plus years, most of the tech problems we've run into weren't really technology problems. A process grew sideways, a few tools overlapped, and nobody had time to sort it out. That's the messy middle, and it's where we do our best work.

We explain things plainly, recommend only what actually helps, and tell you if something isn't worth it. If the whole problem is one annoying spreadsheet, we'd rather fix that than sell you something bigger. We're based in Lakeland, Florida, and we work with businesses and nonprofits anywhere.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need to know what I need?

No. Working out what you need is the first conversation. Bring the problem, not the solution.

Will you tell me to replace my tools?

Only if it saves you money or time. Usually the answer is to use what you have better.

What about all the AI hype?

We'll tell you when AI is the right tool and when it's a distraction. Sometimes the answer is a better spreadsheet.
