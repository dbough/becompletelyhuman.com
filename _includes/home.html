Why Completely Human

The messy middle is where it happens.

I wanted to be a comic book artist. Then I spent my twenties bouncing between accounting, sociology, and zookeeping before I landed in tech. I've now spent more than twenty years there, and I've done a bit of everything: tech support, networks, phone systems, contact centers, sysadmin work, and thirteen years building software and leading engineering teams.

None of it was a straight line, and every detour taught me something I still use.

To me, being completely human means embracing the messy middle: the stretch between a great idea or a problem worth solving and the final solution. That's where the actual work gets done, and it's where we help you.

Our goal is to help you be successful, and being completely human is how we do it.

Dan Bough, founder
