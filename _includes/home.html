Why Completely Human

Because pretending is exhausting.

I wanted to be a comic book artist. Then I spent my twenties bouncing between accounting, sociology, and zookeeping before I landed in tech, where I stayed for more than twenty years building software and leading engineering teams.

None of that was a straight line, and I've stopped pretending it was. Completely Human is where I do the work I care about now, out in the open: coaching, consulting, building things, making things, writing. Some of it will work. Some of it won't. I'll learn from both, and I'll probably tell you about it.

I still get imposter syndrome. Most people I know do too. They just don't say it. I'd rather say it and then do the work anyway. That's the whole idea.

Dan Bough, founder
