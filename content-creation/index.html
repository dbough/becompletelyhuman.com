Why in public

The messy middle works better on camera.

I'm redesigning the next stretch of my life out loud, and that's the content. Not a polished highlight reel. The part in the middle, where the idea isn't a result yet and I don't know how it ends.

People tend to stick around for that. It's easier to trust someone who shows you the whole process, including the parts that didn't work, and that trust is what a brand is really borrowing when it works with us.
