What a session feels like

Less advice. More clarity.

A session is 45 minutes, one on one with Dan, usually on video. You bring whatever is on your mind. Every conversation is its own thing.

We don't hand you a plan or tell you what to do. We slow things down, ask better questions, and stay with you until something shifts. Then you pick what you'll try next, and next time you tell us how it went.

Sometimes that ends with a clear plan. Sometimes it's just saying the thing out loud for the first time. Both count.

Fit

An honest look at who this works for.

Good fit

  • You're ready to invest in yourself, in time and effort as well as money.
  • You'll try things between sessions and report back honestly.
  • You want a partner, not a cheerleader or a lecturer.

Not a fit

  • You want someone to hand you the answers.
  • You plan to show up to sessions and change nothing in between.
  • You're looking for therapy or mental health treatment. Coaching isn't that.

How it starts

Three steps, no pressure.

  1. A free 30 minute call

    No commitment, no agenda. Tell us what's going on and see whether it feels like a fit.

  2. Pick a package

    Most people start with a package of sessions, so there's room for real change. Ask us about current rates.

  3. Do the work

    Sessions are where we think it through. The change happens in the days between.
