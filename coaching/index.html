Coaching

Grow on your own terms.

Something needs to change, and you get to decide what. "I don't know yet" is a fine place to start. We partner with you, bring radical curiosity, and keep the goals, the agenda and the topics in your hands.

Book a free intro call Is this for me?

What a session feels like

Less advice. More clarity.

A session is 45 minutes, one on one with Dan. Bring whatever is on your mind, even if it's still a mess. We go at your pace.

We don't hand you a plan or tell you what to do. We slow things down, ask better questions, and stay with you until something shifts. Sometimes you leave with something to try. Sometimes you just said the thing out loud for the first time. Next time, you share what you learned.

Fit

An honest look at who this works for.

Good fit

  • You're ready to put real time and effort into it.
  • You'll try things between sessions and share what you learned.
  • You want someone in your corner who will cheer you on and still ask the hard questions.

Not a fit

  • You want someone to hand you the answers.
  • You want to talk about change without trying anything different.
  • You're looking for therapy or mental health treatment. Coaching isn't that.

How it starts

Three steps, no pressure.

  1. A free 30 minute call

    No commitment, no agenda. Tell us what's going on and see whether it feels like a fit.

  2. Pick a package

    Most people start with three or six sessions. If cost is what's in the way, say so and we'll work something out.

  3. Do the work

    Sessions are where we think it through. The change happens in the days between.
